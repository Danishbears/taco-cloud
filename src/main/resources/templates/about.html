WHAT IS TACO CLOUD?

A taco experience
built around you.

Taco Cloud is designed to make creating a taco simple, interactive and fun. Instead of choosing from a fixed menu, you can build your own combination from a selection of wraps, proteins, cheeses, vegetables and sauces.

Once your taco is ready, you can give it a name, add it to your order and make changes whenever you want. The goal is simple: give you complete control over what ends up in your taco.

BUILT WITH

A small application
with a modern backend.

Taco Cloud is built as a Spring Boot web application with Thymeleaf used to render dynamic pages. The application combines server-side templates, forms, validation and application logic to provide the complete taco-building experience.
